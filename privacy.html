Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Plain-language summary: Pocket Credit saves the information you choose to enter so you can use the tool and sync your progress. It uses Supabase for accounts and saved data, and Google Gemini may receive limited planning context to generate Advisor responses.

1. Who operates Pocket Credit

Pocket Credit is operated by Sean Maclean. For privacy questions, contact: [add your email address].

2. Information collected

Pocket Credit may collect:

  • Your Google account identifier, email address, and display name when you sign in.
  • Credentials, progress statuses, notes, and other information you choose to save.
  • Advisor questions and limited conversation history needed to respond to your request.
  • Basic technical information processed by hosting and database providers, which may include IP address and request data.

3. How information is used

Your information is used to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Save and display your self-reported progress.
  • Provide Advisor responses and pathway checklists.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the service.

4. Service providers

Pocket Credit uses third-party services to operate, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, and Edge Functions.
  • Google for Google Sign-In and, where applicable, Gemini AI services.
  • Cloudflare for website hosting and delivery.

These providers may process information necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

5. AI processing

When you use the Advisor, Pocket Credit may send limited catalog information, your self-reported progress, and your question or recent conversation to Google Gemini to generate a response.

Do not enter sensitive personal information that you do not want processed by AI services. Avoid entering passwords, financial details, health information, or documents you do not have permission to share.

6. Data retention

Saved profile information remains stored while your account is active or until you delete it. You can use Clear Local Progress to remove locally stored progress from that browser. Account deletion or data-removal requests can be made by contacting the operator.

7. Your choices

You may use much of Pocket Credit without signing in, though saved progress may remain local to that browser. If you sign in, you can sign out at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by contacting the operator.

8. Children

Pocket Credit is intended for general users and is not directed to children. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are under the age required by your local law without permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

This notice may be updated as Pocket Credit changes. The date above shows when it was last updated.
